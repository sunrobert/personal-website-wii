About Me

A little about who I am

Hi, I'm Robert

I'm a 22-year-old currently living in Atlanta. I was born in Canada but spent most of my life moving between China, the U.S., and Canada.

Currently, I'm part of the Base UXR Operations team at Coinbase (NASDAQ: COIN), supporting research initiatives that help make crypto trusted and easy to use.

Beyond work, I run Blockchain at Emory, a student-led organization of 50+ members exploring blockchain technology and its applications. I also serve as a Board Member at the Blockchain Acceleration Foundation, a 501(c)(3) nonprofit dedicated to advancing the growth of a user-owned internet.

Previously, I worked at Bitmind, where I focused on decentralized AI and the Bittensor ecosystem, helping build open, community-driven AI infrastructure.
